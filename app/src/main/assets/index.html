Block Claim

Roll two dice, place your block, wall off the paper.

Play

Pass one phone back and forth. Works offline.

Players
Grid
Game mode

Golden squares are scattered at random but mirrored across the board, so each player gets the same layout from their corner. An odd number puts one in the centre, which keeps ties unlikely.

Rules

How to play
  • Player 1's first block must cover the top-left square; Player 2's must cover the bottom-right.
  • On your turn, roll two dice. A 4 and a 3 gives a 4×3 block; rotate it either way.
  • Every later block must share an edge with one of your own blocks. No overlaps, no hanging off the paper.
  • If your roll can't fit anywhere, you lose a life (3 by default). At zero lives you're out and your opponent keeps rolling.
  • Empty space only you can reach is sealed: it fills in with your colour and counts toward your score.
  • The game ends when no empty space can be reached by both players, or when both are out. Empty space only you can reach counts as yours.
  • Power Squares mode: only golden squares score. Capture one by covering it with a block or sealing it inside your space. Most gold wins; total squares break a tie.
  • Online: one player picks Host online and shares the 4-letter code; the other picks Join online and types it in. Each phone can only move on its own turn.
  • Board: pinch to zoom, drag to look around. Tap to drop your block, drag it to move it, double-tap to rotate. Confirm with Place.
