How to play

  1. Corners. Player 1's first block covers the top-left square; Player 2's covers the bottom-right.
  2. Roll. Two dice make your block: a 4 and a 3 is a 4×3, either way round.
  3. Place. Every block after the first must share an edge with one of yours. No overlaps, nothing off the paper.
  4. Board. Tap to drop the block, drag it to move, double-tap to rotate, pinch to zoom. Tap Place to confirm.
  5. No fit? You lose a life (3 by default). At zero you're out and your opponent plays on.
  6. Sealed space. Empty space only you can reach fills in with your colour and counts as yours.
  7. End. When no space is left that both players can reach, or both are out. Most squares wins.
  8. Power Squares. Only golden squares score. Cover one or seal it inside your space to capture it.
  9. Online. Host shares a 4-letter code; the other player joins with it. Each phone moves on its own turn.
