How to play

  1. Corners. Up to four players. Each first block covers your corner: Blue top-left, Red bottom-right, Yellow top-right, Green bottom-left. Play goes in that order.
  2. Roll. Two dice make your block: a 4 and a 3 is a 4×3, either way round.
  3. Place. Every block after the first must share an edge with one of yours. No overlaps, nothing off the paper.
  4. Board. Tap to drop the block, drag it to move, double-tap to rotate, pinch to zoom. Tap Place to confirm.
  5. Rerolls. Each player gets a set number of rerolls per game (1 by default). Use one on any roll before placing it, including one that won't fit. ↻ on a score card shows how many are left.
  6. No fit? You lose a life (3 by default). At zero you're out and everyone else plays on.
  7. Sealed space. Empty space only you can reach fills in with your colour and counts as yours.
  8. End. When no space is left that two or more players can reach, or everyone is out. Most squares wins.
  9. Layouts. Specialty boards have blacked-out squares nobody can use. Doorways can be 1 square wide, and only a block with a side that small fits through, so a big roll isn't always better.
  10. Power Squares. Only golden squares score. Cover one or seal it inside your space to capture it. They land somewhere new each game.
  11. Rematch. Who goes first moves along one seat each rematch.
  12. Computer. Add up to three, each Easy, Medium or Hard. Easy plays loosely. Medium races for space nearby. Hard reads the whole board, walls players in and squeezes the room they need for their next roll.
  13. Online. The host shares a 4-letter code and up to three players join with it, then the host taps Start. Each phone moves on its own turn.
